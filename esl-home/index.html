Speak Real English — Audio Home

Free audio for the book Speak Real English — A2–B1 English for Real Life. Pick a unit to hear its dialogue (slow + natural speed) and its words, and to shadow it: listen, repeat, record yourself, play back.

Trainers

  • Minimal-pair trainer — ship / sheep, full / fool. Tap to hear, tap to choose which word you heard.
  • Check my sentence — type a sentence from an exercise and get instant right / wrong + the correct form.

All 30 units

  1. Unit 1 — Hello! Nice to meet you. (Your first day at a new job. You meet a colleague.)
  2. Unit 2 — A normal day at work (You talk with a colleague about your daily routine.)
  3. Unit 3 — Calling about a problem (You phone a company because your internet is not working.)
  4. Unit 4 — At the doctor (You feel ill. You see a doctor and explain the problem.)
  5. Unit 5 — At the pharmacy (You take a prescription to the pharmacy and ask for advice.)
  6. Unit 6 — Buying clothes - and taking them back (You buy a shirt, but it is too small. You take it back to the shop.)
  7. Unit 7 — At the supermarket (You cannot find things in a big supermarket. You ask a member of staff.)
  8. Unit 8 — At the restaurant (You order food in a restaurant. There is a problem with your order.)
  9. Unit 9 — Small talk at a cafe (You meet a neighbour at a cafe. You talk about the weather and the weekend.)
  10. Unit 10 — At the bank (Your bank card does not work. You go to the bank for help.)
  11. Unit 11 — At the airport (You fly to another country. Your flight is late.)
  12. Unit 12 — Checking in at a hotel (You arrive at a hotel. You check in and ask about the room.)
  13. Unit 13 — Asking for directions (You are lost in a new city. You ask a stranger for directions.)
  14. Unit 14 — A job interview (You go to a job interview. You talk about what you can do.)
  15. Unit 15 — Writing a work email (You need a day off work. You write a polite email to your manager.)
  16. Unit 16 — In a meeting (You are in a team meeting. You give your opinion and agree or disagree.)
  17. Unit 17 — Making a complaint (Your neighbours are very noisy at night. You speak to them politely.)
  18. Unit 18 — Renting a flat (You want to rent a flat. You talk to the landlord about the rules.)
  19. Unit 19 — Taking a bus or a train (You buy a ticket and ask about the time of the next train.)
  20. Unit 20 — Talking about the weekend (It is Monday morning. You talk with a colleague about the weekend.)
  21. Unit 21 — Inviting someone - and saying yes or no (You invite a friend to dinner. Your friend says yes, and another friend says no politely.)
  22. Unit 22 — Staying healthy (You join a gym. You talk about healthy habits with a trainer.)
  23. Unit 23 — Talking to your child's teacher (You meet your child's teacher. You ask how your child is doing at school.)
  24. Unit 24 — An emergency - calling for help (You see a small fire in a kitchen. You call for help and stay calm.)
  25. Unit 25 — A delivery problem (You ordered a parcel, but it has not arrived. You phone the shop.)
  26. Unit 26 — Sending a parcel (You send a parcel to your family. You ask about the price and the time.)
  27. Unit 27 — At the hairdresser (You go to the hairdresser. You say how you want your hair cut.)
  28. Unit 28 — Car trouble (Your car makes a strange noise. You take it to a mechanic.)
  29. Unit 29 — At the dentist (Your tooth hurts. You see a dentist and explain the pain.)
  30. Unit 30 — At the library (You join a library. You ask how to borrow books.)
