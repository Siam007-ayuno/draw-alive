Unit 27 — At the hairdresser

You go to the hairdresser. You say how you want your hair cut.

From the book Speak Real English — A2–B1 English for Real Life. The book is complete on paper alone; this page is a free helper.

1. Listen to the dialogue


Slow first, then natural speed. Listen twice before you read along.

Emma: Hello. I would like a haircut, please.

Leo: Hello, Emma. Sit here, please. How would you like it?

Emma: I would like it shorter, please. Not too short - just a little.

Leo: Shorter at the back and longer at the front?

Emma: Yes, that is right. And a little off the top too, please.

Leo: No problem. What do you think of this length?

Emma: That is good. Can you wash it too, please?

Leo: Of course. I will wash it first, and then I will cut it.

Emma: Thank you. That is perfect. How much is it?

Leo: That is twenty pounds. Thank you. See you next month!

2. Say it three ways

“I want a haircut.”
Formal: I would like a haircut, please.
Neutral: I want a haircut, please.
Casual: Haircut, please.

“Make it shorter.”
Formal: Could you make it a little shorter, please?
Neutral: Please make it shorter.
Casual: A bit shorter, please.

“That is perfect.”
Formal: That is perfect, thank you very much.
Neutral: That is perfect. Thank you.
Casual: Lovely, thanks!

3. Words to know

Hear every word, its meaning and an example. Tap “Hear it” for one word.

haircut — when someone cuts your hair
I would like a haircut, please.

shorter — less long
I would like it shorter.

longer — more long
Longer at the front, please.

wash — clean with water
Can you wash it too, please?

length — how long something is
What do you think of this length?

top — the highest part
A little off the top, please.

back — the part behind
Shorter at the back, please.

front — the part in front
Longer at the front, please.
