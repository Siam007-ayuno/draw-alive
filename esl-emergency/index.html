Unit 24 — An emergency - calling for help

You see a small fire in a kitchen. You call for help and stay calm.

From the book Speak Real English — A2–B1 English for Real Life. The book is complete on paper alone; this page is a free helper.

1. Listen to the dialogue


Slow first, then natural speed. Listen twice before you read along.

Nadia: Hello? There is a fire in my kitchen! I need help!

Operator: Stay calm. What is your address, please?

Nadia: My address is 12 Green Street. The fire is small, but there is a lot of smoke.

Operator: Is anyone inside the house?

Nadia: No, we are all outside. My husband is calling the neighbours.

Operator: Good. Do not go back inside. The fire engine is coming now.

Nadia: Thank you. How long will it take?

Operator: About five minutes. Stay outside and wait for the fire engine.

Nadia: I will. Please hurry!

Operator: We are coming. Stay on the phone with me.

2. Say it three ways

“I need help!”
Formal: I need urgent help, please.
Neutral: I need help, please.
Casual: Help! I need help!

“Stay calm.”
Formal: Please try to stay calm.
Neutral: Stay calm, please.
Casual: Take it easy.

“Do not go back inside.”
Formal: Please do not go back inside.
Neutral: Do not go back inside.
Casual: Don't go back in.

3. Words to know

Hear every word, its meaning and an example. Tap “Hear it” for one word.

emergency — a serious problem that needs help now
This is an emergency.

fire — hot flames that burn things
There is a fire in my kitchen!

smoke — grey air that comes from a fire
There is a lot of smoke.

alarm — a loud sound that warns you of danger
The alarm is ringing.

address — the place where you live, with a number and a street
My address is 12 Green Street.

stay calm — do not worry, stay relaxed
Stay calm.

fire engine — a big red vehicle that brings water to a fire
The fire engine is coming now.

outside — not inside a building
Stay outside and wait.
