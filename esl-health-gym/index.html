Unit 22 — Staying healthy

You join a gym. You talk about healthy habits with a trainer.

From the book Speak Real English — A2–B1 English for Real Life. The book is complete on paper alone; this page is a free helper.

1. Listen to the dialogue


Slow first, then natural speed. Listen twice before you read along.

Adam: Hello. I would like to join the gym. I want to be healthy.

Coach Kim: Welcome! How often do you exercise now?

Adam: Not often. I sometimes walk to work, but I never go to the gym.

Coach Kim: That is OK. We will start slowly. Do you smoke?

Adam: No, I never smoke. But I usually eat fast food for lunch.

Coach Kim: You should eat more vegetables. And you should drink more water.

Adam: How many hours should I sleep?

Coach Kim: You should sleep eight hours. And you should come here three times a week.

Adam: Three times a week? That is a lot!

Coach Kim: Start with two times a week. You will feel better in a month.

2. Say it three ways

“You should drink more water.”
Formal: I would suggest you drink more water.
Neutral: You should drink more water.
Casual: Drink more water!

“How often do you exercise?”
Formal: Could you tell me how often you exercise?
Neutral: How often do you exercise?
Casual: How often do you work out?

“I want to be healthy.”
Formal: I would like to improve my health.
Neutral: I want to be healthy.
Casual: I want to get fit.

3. Words to know

Hear every word, its meaning and an example. Tap “Hear it” for one word.

healthy — when your body is well and strong
I want to be healthy.

exercise — activity that makes your body strong
How often do you exercise?

gym — a place where people exercise
I would like to join the gym.

trainer — a person who helps you exercise
The trainer is very kind.

vegetables — plants we eat, like carrots and salad
You should eat more vegetables.

sleep — rest with your eyes closed at night
You should sleep eight hours.

often — many times
How often do you exercise?

slowly — not fast
We will start slowly.
