Unit 6 — Buying clothes - and taking them back

You buy a shirt, but it is too small. You take it back to the shop.

From the book Speak Real English — A2–B1 English for Real Life. The book is complete on paper alone; this page is a free helper.

1. Listen to the dialogue


Slow first, then natural speed. Listen twice before you read along.

Nina: Excuse me. I bought this shirt yesterday, but it is too small.

Dan: No problem. Do you have the receipt?

Nina: Yes, here it is. I would like a bigger size, please.

Dan: Of course. This one is a medium. Would you like a large?

Nina: Yes, please. Is the large more expensive?

Dan: No, it is the same price. Would you like to try it on?

Nina: Yes, please. Where is the changing room?

Dan: It is over there, on the left. Take your time.

Nina: Thank you. This one is much better. It is more comfortable.

Dan: Great! It looks good on you. Shall I put it in a bag?

2. Say it three ways

“I want to return this.”
Formal: I would like to return this, please.
Neutral: I want to return this, please.
Casual: I need to bring this back.

“It is too small.”
Formal: I am afraid it is a little too small for me.
Neutral: It is too small for me.
Casual: It is too small.

“Can I try it on?”
Formal: Could I try this on, please?
Neutral: Can I try it on?
Casual: Can I try this on?

3. Words to know

Hear every word, its meaning and an example. Tap “Hear it” for one word.

receipt — the paper that shows you paid for something
Do you have the receipt?

size — how big or small a piece of clothing is
I would like a bigger size, please.

try on — put on clothes to see if they fit
Would you like to try it on?

changing room — a small room in a shop where you try on clothes
Where is the changing room?

refund — money you get back when you return something
Can I have a refund, please?

exchange — to give something back and take a different one
I would like to exchange this shirt.

comfortable — feeling good and relaxed to wear
This shirt is more comfortable.

fit — to be the right size for your body
This shirt does not fit me.
