Did you know? A gift bag is a paper bag made to carry a present, with handles to hold it.

Draw it step by step

The same 4 steps as the book — follow them at your own speed.

Step 1

Start with a big green bag and a white top edge.

Step 2

Add two handles and fluffy white tissue paper on top.

Step 3

Draw a red bow, two big eyes and a smile on the bag.

Step 4

Add dark green shading and gold stars to finish!

Colour it in!
