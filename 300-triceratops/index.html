Did you know? Triceratops means 'three-horned face' in Greek.

Draw it step by step

The same 4 steps as the book — follow them at your own speed.

Step 1

Start with a big round head and a big oval body.

Step 2

Add four strong legs and a short pointy tail.

Step 3

Draw a big frill behind the head and three horns.

Step 4

Add an eye, a beak and spots on the frill to finish!
