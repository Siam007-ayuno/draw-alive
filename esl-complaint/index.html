Unit 17 — Making a complaint

Your neighbours are very noisy at night. You speak to them politely.

From the book Speak Real English — A2–B1 English for Real Life. The book is complete on paper alone; this page is a free helper.

1. Listen to the dialogue


Slow first, then natural speed. Listen twice before you read along.

David: Hello, Rosa. I am sorry to say this, but I need to talk about the noise.

Rosa: Hello, David. Oh no. Was the music too loud last night?

David: Yes, it was very loud. It played until two in the morning.

Rosa: I am very sorry. We had a party for my sister's birthday.

David: I understand. But I start work at six, and I could not sleep.

Rosa: That is bad. I am sorry. We will not do it again.

David: Thank you. Could you turn the music down after ten, please?

Rosa: Yes, of course. After ten, we will keep it quiet.

David: Thank you very much. I am sorry to complain.

Rosa: No, it is fine. Good neighbours talk about problems. Have a nice day!

2. Say it three ways

“The music is too loud.”
Formal: I am sorry to say this, but the music is a little too loud.
Neutral: The music is too loud.
Casual: The music's too loud.

“Could you turn it down?”
Formal: Could you turn the music down a little, please?
Neutral: Could you turn it down, please?
Casual: Can you turn it down a bit?

“I am sorry to complain.”
Formal: I am sorry to have to mention this.
Neutral: I am sorry to complain.
Casual: Sorry to bring this up.

3. Words to know

Hear every word, its meaning and an example. Tap “Hear it” for one word.

complaint — when you say you are not happy about something
I am sorry to complain.

noise — a loud sound you do not want
I need to talk about the noise.

neighbour — a person who lives near you
Good neighbours talk about problems.

party — a happy time with friends, food and music
We had a party for my sister's birthday.

loud — a sound you can hear very well
The music was very loud.

quiet — with little or no sound
After ten, we will keep it quiet.

turn down — make a sound lower
Could you turn the music down, please?

sleep — rest with your eyes closed at night
I could not sleep.
