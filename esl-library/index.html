Unit 30 — At the library

You join a library. You ask how to borrow books.

From the book Speak Real English — A2–B1 English for Real Life. The book is complete on paper alone; this page is a free helper.

1. Listen to the dialogue


Slow first, then natural speed. Listen twice before you read along.

Chris: Hello. I would like to join the library, please.

Mia: Hello. Welcome! Can I see some ID, please?

Chris: Yes, here it is. Can I borrow books today?

Mia: Yes, you can. You can borrow three books at a time.

Chris: How long can I keep them?

Mia: You can keep them for two weeks. You have to bring them back by the date on the card.

Chris: Can I use the computers here?

Mia: Yes, you can. You can use them for one hour a day.

Chris: Thank you. Can I take this book today?

Mia: Of course. Enjoy your book, Chris!

2. Say it three ways

“Can I borrow this book?”
Formal: Could I borrow this book, please?
Neutral: Can I borrow this book, please?
Casual: Can I take this book?

“How long can I keep it?”
Formal: Could you tell me how long I can keep it, please?
Neutral: How long can I keep it?
Casual: How long can I have it for?

“I would like to join.”
Formal: I would like to become a member, please.
Neutral: I would like to join the library, please.
Casual: I'd like to sign up.

3. Words to know

Hear every word, its meaning and an example. Tap “Hear it” for one word.

library — a place where you can borrow books
I would like to join the library.

borrow — take something and bring it back later
Can I borrow this book?

join — become a member of something
I would like to join the library.

ID — a card that shows who you are
Can I see some ID, please?

member — a person who belongs to a group
You are a member now.

return — bring something back
You have to bring the books back.

computers — machines you use to work or read on the internet
Can I use the computers here?

date — a day, like the 5th of May
Bring them back by the date on the card.
