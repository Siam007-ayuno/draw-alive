Unit 20 — Talking about the weekend

It is Monday morning. You talk with a colleague about the weekend.

From the book Speak Real English — A2–B1 English for Real Life. The book is complete on paper alone; this page is a free helper.

1. Listen to the dialogue


Slow first, then natural speed. Listen twice before you read along.

Jack: Good morning, Elena. How was your weekend?

Elena: It was great, thanks. I went to the park with my children.

Jack: That sounds nice. What did you do there?

Elena: We played football and we ate ice cream. The children had a lot of fun.

Jack: Did you go to the cinema too?

Elena: No, we did not. We stayed at home on Sunday. We watched a film.

Jack: What did you watch?

Elena: We watched a funny film about a dog. What about you? What did you do?

Jack: I stayed at home too. I read a book and I cooked a big lunch.

Elena: That sounds relaxing. Did you buy anything new?

2. Say it three ways

“How was your weekend?”
Formal: How was your weekend? I hope it was good.
Neutral: How was your weekend?
Casual: How was your weekend, then?

“It was great.”
Formal: It was wonderful, thank you.
Neutral: It was great, thanks.
Casual: It was brilliant!

“What did you do?”
Formal: What did you get up to at the weekend?
Neutral: What did you do at the weekend?
Casual: What did you get up to?

3. Words to know

Hear every word, its meaning and an example. Tap “Hear it” for one word.

weekend — Saturday and Sunday
How was your weekend?

cinema — a place where you watch films
Did you go to the cinema?

fun — enjoyment and happiness
The children had a lot of fun.

relaxing — making you feel calm and happy
That sounds relaxing.

cook — make food by heating it
I cooked a big lunch.

stay at home — not go out, remain in your house
We stayed at home on Sunday.

play — do something for fun, like a game
We played football.

watch — look at something for a time, like a film
We watched a film.
