Draw it step by step

The same 4 steps as the book — follow them at your own speed.

Step 1

Start with a big round head, a snout and a big oval body.

Step 2

Add a long pointy tail, two strong legs and tiny arms.

Step 3

Draw two big eyes with brow ridges and a wide mouth with sharp teeth.

Step 4

Add a light tummy, spots and a rosy cheek to finish!

Colour it in!

Tap a colour, then tap a part of the drawing to fill it. Nothing is saved anywhere.
