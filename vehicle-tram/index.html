Did you know? The word tram comes from an old word for a beam, and the first people tram ran in 1807 in Wales.

Draw it step by step

The same 4 steps as the book — follow them at your own speed.

Step 1

Start with a long boxy tram body and a roof on top.

Step 2

Add two small wheels under the tram.

Step 3

Draw eyes at the front, three windows and a zigzag pole on the roof.

Step 4

Add a headlight, a stripe and a rail line to finish!

Colour it in!

Tap a colour, then tap a part of the drawing to fill it. Nothing is saved anywhere.
