Unit 13 — Asking for directions

You are lost in a new city. You ask a stranger for directions.

From the book Speak Real English — A2–B1 English for Real Life. The book is complete on paper alone; this page is a free helper.

1. Listen to the dialogue


Slow first, then natural speed. Listen twice before you read along.

Jon: Excuse me. Could you help me? I am lost.

Aisha: Of course. Where do you want to go?

Jon: I am looking for the museum. Is it far from here?

Aisha: No, it is not far. Go straight on, then turn left at the park.

Jon: Turn left at the park. And then?

Aisha: The museum is on your right, opposite the library.

Jon: Thank you. And is there a cafe near the museum?

Aisha: Yes. There is a small cafe next to the museum, between the bank and the bookshop.

Jon: Perfect. How long does it take to walk there?

Aisha: About ten minutes. Enjoy your visit!

2. Say it three ways

“I am lost.”
Formal: I am afraid I am lost. Could you help me, please?
Neutral: Excuse me, I am lost.
Casual: I'm lost!

“Where is the museum?”
Formal: Could you tell me where the museum is, please?
Neutral: Where is the museum, please?
Casual: Where's the museum?

“Thank you very much.”
Formal: Thank you very much for your help.
Neutral: Thank you very much.
Casual: Thanks a lot!

3. Words to know

Hear every word, its meaning and an example. Tap “Hear it” for one word.

lost — not knowing where you are
I am lost.

straight on — forward, without turning
Go straight on.

turn left — go to the left side
Turn left at the park.

opposite — on the other side of a road or place
The museum is opposite the library.

between — in the middle of two things
The cafe is between the bank and the bookshop.

traffic lights — lights that tell cars to stop or go
Turn right at the traffic lights.

corner — where two streets meet
The shop is on the corner.

near — not far away
Is there a cafe near the museum?
