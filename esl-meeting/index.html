Unit 16 — In a meeting

You are in a team meeting. You give your opinion and agree or disagree.

From the book Speak Real English — A2–B1 English for Real Life. The book is complete on paper alone; this page is a free helper.

1. Listen to the dialogue


Slow first, then natural speed. Listen twice before you read along.

Alex: Good morning, everyone. We need to talk about the new working hours.

Sara: I think we should start at nine, not eight. Many people travel far.

Alex: I agree with Sara. The bus is very slow before eight.

Sara: And I think we should finish at five, because the shop closes at six.

Alex: I am afraid I disagree. If we finish at five, we will not finish the orders.

Sara: That is true. What if we start at eight and finish at four on Friday?

Alex: That is a good idea. I think the team will like that.

Sara: Shall we ask the team what they think?

Alex: Yes. I will send an email today, and we will talk about it on Monday.

Sara: Good. Thank you, everyone. The meeting is finished.

2. Say it three ways

“I think this is a good idea.”
Formal: In my opinion, this is an excellent idea.
Neutral: I think this is a good idea.
Casual: I reckon this is a good idea.

“I disagree.”
Formal: I am afraid I disagree with that.
Neutral: I disagree, I am afraid.
Casual: I don't think so.

“Let's ask the team.”
Formal: Shall we ask the team what they think?
Neutral: Let's ask the team.
Casual: Let's see what the team thinks.

3. Words to know

Hear every word, its meaning and an example. Tap “Hear it” for one word.

opinion — what you think about something
What is your opinion?

agree — think the same as someone
I agree with Sara.

disagree — think differently from someone
I am afraid I disagree.

idea — a thought or a plan
That is a good idea.

meeting — a time when people come together to talk about work
We have a meeting on Monday.

team — a group of people who work together
I think the team will like that.

reason — why something is true or why you do something
Give a reason for your opinion.

suggest — say an idea for other people to think about
I suggest we start at nine.
