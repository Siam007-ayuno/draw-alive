Unit 10 — At the bank

Your bank card does not work. You go to the bank for help.

From the book Speak Real English — A2–B1 English for Real Life. The book is complete on paper alone; this page is a free helper.

1. Listen to the dialogue


Slow first, then natural speed. Listen twice before you read along.

Ravi: Good morning. My card does not work. I can't take out money.

Julia: Good morning. I am sorry to hear that. When did it stop working?

Ravi: It stopped yesterday. I tried to pay in a shop, but it did not work.

Julia: Did you try the machine outside?

Ravi: Yes, I did. The machine took my card and did not give it back.

Julia: I see. Did you lose your card before that?

Ravi: No, I did not lose it. I had it in my wallet all day.

Julia: Good. I will order a new card for you. It will arrive in five days.

Ravi: Thank you. Can I take out money today? I need some cash.

Julia: Yes, you can. Please bring your passport, and I will help you at the desk.

2. Say it three ways

“My card does not work.”
Formal: I am afraid my card is not working.
Neutral: My card does not work.
Casual: My card's not working.

“Can I take out money?”
Formal: Could I withdraw some money, please?
Neutral: Can I take out money, please?
Casual: Can I get some cash out?

“I need help.”
Formal: I would appreciate your help with this.
Neutral: I need some help, please.
Casual: I need a hand with this.

3. Words to know

Hear every word, its meaning and an example. Tap “Hear it” for one word.

card — a small plastic card you use to pay or take out money
My card does not work.

cash — money in the form of coins and notes
I need some cash today.

account — an arrangement with a bank to keep your money
Could you check my account, please?

balance — the amount of money in your account
What is my balance?

withdraw — take money out of your account
I want to withdraw fifty pounds.

machine — a machine outside a bank that gives you money
The machine took my card.

passport — a book that proves who you are
Please bring your passport.

arrive — come to a place
The new card will arrive in five days.
