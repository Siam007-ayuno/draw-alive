Unit 9 — Small talk at a cafe

You meet a neighbour at a cafe. You talk about the weather and the weekend.

From the book Speak Real English — A2–B1 English for Real Life. The book is complete on paper alone; this page is a free helper.

1. Listen to the dialogue


Slow first, then natural speed. Listen twice before you read along.

Priya: Hi, Mark! What are you doing here?

Mark: Hi, Priya! I am waiting for a friend. We are going to have lunch.

Priya: Nice. It is cold today, isn't it?

Mark: Yes, it is raining too. I am going to stay at home this evening.

Priya: Me too. What are you doing this weekend?

Mark: I am going to visit my sister. She lives in the next town.

Priya: That sounds nice. How is your new job?

Mark: It is going well. I am learning a lot. How is your family?

Priya: They are all fine. My son is starting school next week.

Mark: That is great news! Well, here is my friend. See you soon, Priya!

2. Say it three ways

“How are you?”
Formal: How are you doing today?
Neutral: How are you?
Casual: How's it going?

“It is cold today.”
Formal: It is rather cold today, isn't it?
Neutral: It is cold today.
Casual: Freezing today!

“See you soon.”
Formal: I look forward to seeing you again soon.
Neutral: See you soon.
Casual: Catch you later!

3. Words to know

Hear every word, its meaning and an example. Tap “Hear it” for one word.

neighbour — a person who lives near you
My neighbour is very friendly.

weather — rain, sun, wind and temperature
The weather is cold today.

weekend — Saturday and Sunday
What are you doing this weekend?

visit — go to see a person or a place
I am going to visit my sister.

sounds — used to say something seems good or nice
That sounds nice.

news — new information about someone or something
That is great news!

learning — getting new knowledge or a new skill
I am learning a lot at my new job.

starting — beginning something new
My son is starting school next week.
