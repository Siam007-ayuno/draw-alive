Did you know? A doll is a toy made to look like a baby or a person, for children to dress and cuddle.

Draw it step by step

The same 4 steps as the book — follow them at your own speed.

Step 1

Start with a round head and a pretty pink dress.

Step 2

Add curly hair, two arms and two little legs.

Step 3

Draw big eyes, a smile and pretty eyelashes.

Step 4

Add a red bow, red shoes and a gold star!

Colour it in!

Tap a colour, then tap a part of the drawing to fill it. Nothing is saved anywhere.
