Draw it in the book!

Start with a big round trailer and a hitch at the front.

Did you know? The word caravan comes from a Persian word for people travelling together, and the first fun trailer was built in 1880.

Draw it step by step

The same 4 steps as the book — follow them at your own speed.

Step 1

Start with a big round trailer and a hitch at the front.

Step 2

Add one big round wheel underneath.

Step 3

Draw two eyes on the front, a window and a door.

Step 4

Add a stripe and a little leg to finish!

Colour it in!

Tap a colour, then tap a part of the drawing to fill it. Nothing is saved anywhere.
