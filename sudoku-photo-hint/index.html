How the hint works

  • Easy puzzles only need singles — a cell that can only be one number, or a number that can only go in one cell.
  • Medium puzzles also use pairs and pointing — two cells sharing two numbers, or a number trapped in one row of a box.
  • Hard puzzles add triples, box-line reduction and the X-wing — a rectangle of four cells.
  • Championship Challenge puzzles use the same Hard techniques — they are the hardest puzzles in the book.
  • The hint always shows the easiest next step first, and explains why — so you learn the thinking, not just the answer.
