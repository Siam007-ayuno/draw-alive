Unit 11 — At the airport

You fly to another country. Your flight is late.

From the book Speak Real English — A2–B1 English for Real Life. The book is complete on paper alone; this page is a free helper.

1. Listen to the dialogue


Slow first, then natural speed. Listen twice before you read along.

Hana: Excuse me. Is this the queue for flight BA 204 to Paris?

Paul: Yes, it is. Are you going to Paris too?

Hana: Yes, I am. I am going to visit my brother. He lives there.

Paul: Nice. I am going to Paris for work. I am going to stay for two days.

Hana: What time does the flight leave?

Paul: It leaves at ten, but it is late. It will leave at half past eleven.

Hana: Oh no. Will we miss our connection?

Paul: No, do not worry. The company will change your ticket for free.

Hana: That is good. I will call my brother when we land.

Paul: Good idea. I will wait with you until the gate opens.

2. Say it three ways

“Is the flight on time?”
Formal: Could you tell me if the flight is on time, please?
Neutral: Is the flight on time?
Casual: Is it on time?

“I need help with my ticket.”
Formal: I would appreciate some help with my ticket, please.
Neutral: I need help with my ticket.
Casual: Can you help me with my ticket?

“Do not worry.”
Formal: Please do not worry about it.
Neutral: Do not worry.
Casual: No worries.

3. Words to know

Hear every word, its meaning and an example. Tap “Hear it” for one word.

flight — a journey by plane
My flight leaves at ten.

gate — the place in an airport where you get on the plane
The gate opens at eleven.

delay — when something is later than planned
There is a delay of one hour.

connection — a second flight after the first one
Will we miss our connection?

ticket — a paper or phone screen that lets you travel
The company will change your ticket.

passport control — the place where officers check your passport
Go through passport control first.

luggage — the bags you take when you travel
My luggage is very heavy.

land — when a plane comes down to the ground
I will call you when we land.
