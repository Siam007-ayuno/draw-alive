Draw it in the book!

Did you know? A raccoon can climb down a tree head first, thanks to bendy ankles.

Draw it step by step

The same 4 steps as the book — follow them at your own speed.

Step 1

Start with a big circle for the head and an oval for the body.

Step 2

Add two pointy ears and a black mask across the eyes.

Step 3

Draw two eyes, a light muzzle and a tiny nose.

Step 4

Add a stripy tail, a tummy and paws to finish!

Colour it in!

Tap a colour, then tap a part of the drawing to fill it. Nothing is saved anywhere.
