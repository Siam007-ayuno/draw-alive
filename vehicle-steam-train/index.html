Draw it step by step

The same 4 steps as the book — follow them at your own speed.

Step 1

Start with a big round boiler at the front and a cab at the back.

Step 2

Add four round wheels under the train.

Step 3

Draw two eyes on the boiler, a tall funnel and puffs of smoke.

Step 4

Add a window, a stripe and a cowcatcher at the front to finish!

Colour it in!

Tap a colour, then tap a part of the drawing to fill it. Nothing is saved anywhere.
