Unit 3 — Calling about a problem

You phone a company because your internet is not working.

From the book Speak Real English — A2–B1 English for Real Life. The book is complete on paper alone; this page is a free helper.

1. Listen to the dialogue


Slow first, then natural speed. Listen twice before you read along.

Mina: Hello, this is Mina. Could I speak to someone about my internet?

Alex: Of course. This is Alex. How can I help you?

Mina: My internet is not working. It stopped yesterday evening.

Alex: I am sorry to hear that. Could you tell me your account number, please?

Mina: Yes, it is four five six seven. The light on the box is red.

Alex: Thank you. Could you hold on for a minute, please? I will check.

Mina: Of course. Take your time.

Alex: Thank you for waiting. There is a problem in your street. We will fix it today.

Mina: That is good. Could you call me back when it is fixed?

Alex: Yes, I will call you this afternoon. Sorry again for the problem.

2. Say it three ways

“I want to speak to Sara.”
Formal: Could I speak to Sara, please?
Neutral: Can I speak to Sara, please?
Casual: Is Sara there?

“Please wait a minute.”
Formal: Could you hold on for a moment, please?
Neutral: Please hold on for a minute.
Casual: One sec, please.

“Call me later.”
Formal: Could you call me back later, please?
Neutral: Please call me back later.
Casual: Give me a call later.

3. Words to know

Hear every word, its meaning and an example. Tap “Hear it” for one word.

account number — the number a company gives you so they can find you
Could you tell me your account number, please?

hold on — wait for a short time on the phone
Could you hold on for a minute, please?

call back — phone someone again later
I will call you back this afternoon.

message — words you leave for someone who is not there
Please leave a message after the sound.

voicemail — a recorded message on a phone
I left a voicemail for the doctor.

busy — when a phone line is in use, or a person has a lot to do
The line is busy. Please try again.

fix — make something work again
We will fix the internet today.

line — a phone connection
The line is busy at the moment.
