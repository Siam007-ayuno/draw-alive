Unit 1 — Hello! Nice to meet you.

Your first day at a new job. You meet a colleague.

From the book Speak Real English — A2–B1 English for Real Life. The book is complete on paper alone; this page is a free helper.

1. Listen to the dialogue


Slow first, then natural speed. Listen twice before you read along.

Anna: Hello! I am Anna. I am new here. I work in the kitchen.

Ben: Hello, Anna. I am Ben. Nice to meet you. I work at the front desk.

Anna: Nice to meet you too, Ben. Are you from this city?

Ben: Yes, I am. I live near the station. What about you?

Anna: I live in the north of the city. I take the bus to work.

Ben: The bus is slow in the morning. I walk to work. It takes ten minutes.

Anna: That is good. What time do you start work?

Ben: I start at eight. We finish at four. The team is friendly here.

Anna: That is good to hear. I am happy to be here.

Ben: Welcome, Anna. If you need help, just ask me.

2. Say it three ways

“I need help.”
Formal: Could you help me, please?
Neutral: Can you help me?
Casual: Could you give me a hand?

“I do not understand.”
Formal: I am sorry, I do not understand. Could you say that again, please?
Neutral: Sorry, I do not understand.
Casual: Sorry - what was that?

“Nice to meet you.”
Formal: It is a pleasure to meet you.
Neutral: Nice to meet you.
Casual: Great to meet you!

3. Words to know

Hear every word, its meaning and an example. Tap “Hear it” for one word.

colleague — a person you work with
My colleague Ben works at the front desk.

manager — the person who is in charge at work
Our manager is kind and fair.

shift — a period of work, for example 8 to 4
My shift starts at eight in the morning.

team — a group of people who work together
Our team has six people.

office — a place where people work at desks
I work in a small office.

break — a short rest from work
We have a break at eleven.

station — a place where trains or buses stop
I live near the station.

welcome — a friendly greeting to a new person
Welcome to our team, Anna!
