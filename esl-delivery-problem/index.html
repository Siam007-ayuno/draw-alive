Unit 25 — A delivery problem

You ordered a parcel, but it has not arrived. You phone the shop.

From the book Speak Real English — A2–B1 English for Real Life. The book is complete on paper alone; this page is a free helper.

1. Listen to the dialogue


Slow first, then natural speed. Listen twice before you read along.

Zoe: Hello. I ordered a parcel on Monday, but I have not received it yet.

Tom: I am sorry to hear that. What is your order number, please?

Zoe: It is eight seven four two. I have already paid for it.

Tom: Thank you. Let me check. Yes, we have sent it. It left our shop yesterday.

Zoe: But it has not arrived yet. Have you sent it to the right address?

Tom: Yes, we have. The address is 45 Hill Road. Is that right?

Zoe: Yes, that is right. When will it arrive?

Tom: It will arrive tomorrow. I am sorry for the delay.

Zoe: Thank you. Could you send me a message when it arrives?

Tom: Of course. I will send you a message tomorrow morning.

2. Say it three ways

“I have not received my parcel.”
Formal: I am writing to let you know that I have not yet received my parcel.
Neutral: I have not received my parcel yet.
Casual: My parcel hasn't turned up yet.

“When will it arrive?”
Formal: Could you tell me when it will arrive, please?
Neutral: When will it arrive?
Casual: When's it coming?

“I am sorry for the delay.”
Formal: I do apologise for the delay.
Neutral: I am sorry for the delay.
Casual: Sorry about the wait.

3. Words to know

Hear every word, its meaning and an example. Tap “Hear it” for one word.

parcel — a box with something inside, sent by post
I ordered a parcel on Monday.

order number — a number that shows which order is yours
What is your order number?

receive — get something that someone sends you
I have not received it yet.

delay — when something is later than planned
I am sorry for the delay.

send — post something to a person or place
We have sent it.

address — the place where you live
The address is 45 Hill Road.

arrive — come to a place
It will arrive tomorrow.

already — before now
I have already paid for it.
