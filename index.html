Chapter 1 • How it works

The QR magic

Every drawing in our books has a little code. Scan it — and the drawing comes alive.

1
Find the code

On the practice page of your book, in a bordered badge.

2
Scan with a phone

Just the camera. No app, no account, no extra cost.

3
Watch it draw

The drawing draws itself, stroke by stroke, with its real sound.

4
Hear & colour

A friendly voice tells a true fact — then tap to colour it on screen.

Honest note: the code shows the book's drawing moving. It does not make your own drawing come alive — that part is yours, with your crayons.

Chapter 8 • About

Made by a student, with code & care

Hi — I'm MD. Saif Siam, a computer science student. I make these books with code and a lot of care: every drawing is built step by step, so any kid can follow it — and every QR code is a small video I made, with real sounds and a friendly voice.

Draw Alive started with one simple idea: a drawing book should not just sit still. It should move, make sounds, and teach something true. That idea is now five books — and more are coming, including puzzle books for grown-ups.
