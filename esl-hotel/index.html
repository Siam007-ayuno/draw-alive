Unit 12 — Checking in at a hotel

You arrive at a hotel. You check in and ask about the room.

From the book Speak Real English — A2–B1 English for Real Life. The book is complete on paper alone; this page is a free helper.

1. Listen to the dialogue


Slow first, then natural speed. Listen twice before you read along.

Mia: Good evening. I have a reservation. My name is Mia Chen.

Noah: Good evening, Ms Chen. Welcome. Could I see your passport, please?

Mia: Yes, here it is. Is there a lift to the third floor?

Noah: Yes, there is. Your room is on the third floor. There are two beds in the room.

Mia: Thank you. Is there a restaurant in the hotel?

Noah: Yes, there is. Breakfast is from seven to ten in the morning.

Mia: Could I have an extra towel, please?

Noah: Of course. I will send one to your room. What time would you like breakfast?

Mia: At eight, please. And could you wake me at seven?

Noah: Yes, of course. Enjoy your stay, Ms Chen.

2. Say it three ways

“I have a reservation.”
Formal: I have a reservation under the name Mia Chen.
Neutral: I have a reservation. My name is Mia Chen.
Casual: I've booked a room - Mia Chen.

“Can I have an extra towel?”
Formal: Could I have an extra towel, please?
Neutral: Can I have an extra towel, please?
Casual: Could I get an extra towel?

“Wake me at seven.”
Formal: Could you wake me at seven, please?
Neutral: Please wake me at seven.
Casual: Give me a wake-up call at seven.

3. Words to know

Hear every word, its meaning and an example. Tap “Hear it” for one word.

reservation — an arrangement to have a room kept for you
I have a reservation.

reception — the desk in a hotel where you check in
Please go to reception.

towel — a piece of cloth you use to dry yourself
Could I have an extra towel, please?

key card — a card that opens your hotel room door
Here is your key card.

stay — the time you spend in a hotel
Enjoy your stay.

wake me — call me so I get up at a time
Could you wake me at seven?

extra — one more than usual
Could I have an extra towel?

floor — a level of a building
Your room is on the third floor.
