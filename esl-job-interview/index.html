Unit 14 — A job interview

You go to a job interview. You talk about what you can do.

From the book Speak Real English — A2–B1 English for Real Life. The book is complete on paper alone; this page is a free helper.

1. Listen to the dialogue


Slow first, then natural speed. Listen twice before you read along.

Fatima: Good morning. Thank you for seeing me today.

Mr Green: Good morning, Fatima. Please sit down. Tell me about yourself.

Fatima: I have worked in a shop for two years. I can speak English and Arabic.

Mr Green: That is good. Can you use a computer?

Fatima: Yes, I can. I am good at Excel and email. I have studied business at college.

Mr Green: Why are you interested in this job?

Fatima: I like working with people. I am a fast learner and I work well in a team.

Mr Green: Can you work on Saturday?

Fatima: Yes, I can. I can work on Saturday and Sunday.

Mr Green: Thank you, Fatima. We will call you next week.

2. Say it three ways

“I can do this job.”
Formal: I believe I am well suited to this role.
Neutral: I can do this job well.
Casual: I can totally do this.

“Thank you for seeing me.”
Formal: Thank you very much for your time today.
Neutral: Thank you for seeing me today.
Casual: Thanks for your time!

“I would like this job.”
Formal: I would be very interested in this position.
Neutral: I would like this job.
Casual: I'd love this job.

3. Words to know

Hear every word, its meaning and an example. Tap “Hear it” for one word.

interview — a meeting where people ask you questions about a job
I have a job interview tomorrow.

experience — the things you have done and learned in work
I have two years of experience.

skill — something you are able to do well
Cooking is one of my skills.

team — a group of people who work together
I work well in a team.

fast learner — a person who learns new things quickly
I am a fast learner.

shift — a period of work
Can you work the evening shift?

salary — the money you get for your job
What is the salary for this job?

apply — ask for a job in writing
I would like to apply for this job.
