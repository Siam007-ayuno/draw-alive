Unit 15 — Writing a work email

You need a day off work. You write a polite email to your manager.

From the book Speak Real English — A2–B1 English for Real Life. The book is complete on paper alone; this page is a free helper.

1. Listen to the dialogue


Slow first, then natural speed. Listen twice before you read along.

Lena: Dear Ms Patel, I hope you are well.

Ms Patel: Dear Lena, I am well, thank you. How can I help you?

Lena: Could I take Friday off, please? I have a doctor's appointment.

Ms Patel: Of course. Would it be possible to finish your report on Thursday?

Lena: Yes, of course. I will finish it on Thursday morning.

Ms Patel: Thank you. Please send me the report by email when it is ready.

Lena: I will do that. Thank you for your help.

Ms Patel: You are welcome. I hope the appointment goes well.

Lena: Thank you. Best wishes, Lena.

Ms Patel: Best wishes, Ms Patel.

2. Say it three ways

“I want Friday off.”
Formal: Could I take Friday off, please?
Neutral: I would like to take Friday off, please.
Casual: Can I have Friday off?

“Send me the report.”
Formal: Could you send me the report, please?
Neutral: Please send me the report.
Casual: Can you send me the report?

“Thank you for your help.”
Formal: Thank you very much for your help.
Neutral: Thank you for your help.
Casual: Thanks for your help!

3. Words to know

Hear every word, its meaning and an example. Tap “Hear it” for one word.

email — a message you send on a computer or phone
Please send me the report by email.

manager — the person who is in charge at work
I wrote an email to my manager.

appointment — a time you agree to meet someone
I have a doctor's appointment.

report — a written paper about your work
I will finish the report on Thursday.

day off — a day when you do not work
Could I take Friday off, please?

subject — the short line that says what an email is about
Write a clear subject line.

attach — add a file to an email
I will attach the report to the email.

reply — answer an email or a message
Please reply to this email.
